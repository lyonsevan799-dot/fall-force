Fall Force Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the browser game Fall Force handles information. Fall Force is a small game and does not ask players to create an account or provide their name, email address, or other contact details to play.

Information stored on your device

The game may use your browser's local storage to save game progress or preferences on your device. This information generally stays in your browser and is not sent to the game developer by the local-storage feature itself. Clearing browser data or using another browser or device may remove or not transfer these saves.

CrazyGames and other third-party services

When you play Fall Force on CrazyGames, CrazyGames and its platform technology may process technical information such as browser/device details, gameplay events, diagnostics, or usage information as described in CrazyGames' own privacy information. Any services enabled by the platform may handle information under their own policies. Please review the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

Fall Force does not currently ask you to submit personal information directly. Platform features, settings, or future game updates may change what third-party services are used; this policy should be updated if the game's data practices change.

Advertising

Advertising features may be controlled by the hosting platform. If ads or advertising-related features are enabled for the game in the future, the platform or its advertising partners may process information according to their policies and settings. Check the hosting platform's privacy information for details.

Children's privacy

Fall Force does not intentionally ask children to provide personal information. If you believe personal information has been submitted directly to the developer in connection with the game, please contact the developer through the game's hosting platform so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the game or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the update date shown at the top.

Contact

For questions about Fall Force and this policy, use the contact or support options provided by the platform where you play the game.
